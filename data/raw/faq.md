# Sunrise Dental: clinic information

All details below are invented for a demo. Each "##" section is one searchable piece of
information. Keep each section about one topic, and write it as plain sentences.

## Opening hours
Sunrise Dental is open Monday to Friday from 8am to 6pm, and on Saturday from 9am to 1pm.
The clinic is closed on Sundays and on bank holidays.
People also ask: when are you open, what time do you open, what time do you close, working hours.

## Prices
A routine check-up costs 45 pounds. A scale and polish with the hygienist costs 60 pounds.
White fillings start from 95 pounds. A new patient examination, including X-rays, costs 70 pounds.
The dentist will always give you a written price before any treatment starts.
People also ask: how much does it cost, what are your fees, what do you charge.

## Treatments and services
What we offer: Sunrise Dental offers routine check-ups, scale and polish with the hygienist, white fillings,
new patient examinations, children's check-ups, and emergency appointments for patients in pain.
To book any of these, just ask to book an appointment.
People also ask: what services do you provide, what treatments are available, what can I get done at the clinic.

## Insurance and payment
We accept most major dental insurance plans. Please bring your policy details to your appointment.
We take card payments, contactless, and cash. Payment is due on the day of treatment.
We offer a monthly payment plan for treatments over 500 pounds.

## Cancellations
Please give at least 24 hours notice if you need to cancel or move an appointment.
You can cancel by calling the clinic or replying to your reminder text message.
Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours notice, or missed, may be charged a fee of 25 pounds.

## Location and parking
Where to find us: the clinic is at 24 Harbour Road, Brightwell, BW1 2AB, next to the post office.
There is free parking for patients behind the building, and a bus stop directly outside.
The clinic has step-free access and a ground-floor treatment room.
People also ask: where are you, where are you located, what is your address, location, how do I get there, directions.

## Children
We welcome children of all ages. Check-ups for children under 18 are free.
We recommend a child's first visit by their first birthday. A parent or guardian must attend with children under 16.

## Emergency appointments
We keep emergency appointment slots free every weekday morning for patients in pain.
To get an emergency slot, call the clinic at 8am. Our urgent dental line is 01632 960 111.
When the clinic is closed, please call NHS 111 for urgent dental help.

## New patients
We are accepting new patients. To register, book a new patient examination by phone or at reception.
Please bring a list of any medicines you take and your previous dentist's details if you have them.

## Phone number and email
To call us, the phone number is 01632 960 000. You can also email hello at sunrise dental dot example.
