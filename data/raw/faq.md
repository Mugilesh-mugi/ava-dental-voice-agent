## Opening hours
Sunrise Dental is open Monday to Friday from 8am to 6pm, and on Saturday from 9am to 1pm.
The clinic is closed on Sundays and on bank holidays.

A tooth extraction starts from 90 pounds. A crown starts from 450 pounds.
Root canal treatment starts from 280 pounds. Teeth whitening costs 250 pounds. A single X-ray costs 25 pounds.

## Treatments and services
What we offer: Sunrise Dental offers routine check-ups, new patient examinations, scale and polish with the hygienist,
white fillings, tooth extractions, crowns, root canal treatment, teeth whitening, X-rays, children's check-ups,
and emergency appointments for patients in pain.
To book any of these, just ask to book an appointment.
People also ask: what services do you provide, what treatments are available, what can I get done at the clinic, what other services do you offer.

## Check-up
A routine check-up is a visit where the dentist looks at your teeth, gums and mouth to spot any problems early.
It takes about 20 to 30 minutes and costs 45 pounds. Most patients come every six months, and the dentist will tell you
how often suits you. A new patient examination costs 70 pounds and includes X-rays.
People also ask: what happens at a check-up, how long is a check-up, how often should I come.

## Scale and polish
A scale and polish is a professional clean with the hygienist. They remove plaque and tartar from your teeth and polish
the surface. It takes about 30 minutes and costs 60 pounds. Your gums may feel a little tender for a day afterwards.
People also ask: what is a scale and polish, teeth cleaning, hygienist, how long does cleaning take.

## Fillings
A filling repairs a tooth that has a cavity. The dentist removes the decayed part and fills the space with a tooth-coloured
white filling. It usually takes 30 to 45 minutes. White fillings start from 95 pounds, and the dentist will give you the exact
price before starting. The area is numbed first so you should not feel pain.
People also ask: what is a filling, white filling, cavity treatment, does a filling hurt.

## Tooth extraction
An extraction is the removal of a tooth that cannot be saved. The area is numbed first, and it usually takes 20 to 40 minutes.
Extractions start from 90 pounds. The dentist will explain what to expect afterwards and give you written aftercare.
People also ask: tooth removal, pull a tooth, wisdom tooth removal, how much is an extraction.

## Crowns
A crown is a cover that fits over a damaged tooth to protect it and restore its shape. It normally takes two visits
about two weeks apart. Crowns start from 450 pounds, and the dentist will give you a written price first.
People also ask: what is a crown, cap, how long does a crown take, how much is a crown.

## Root canal treatment
Root canal treatment saves a tooth whose inner part, the pulp, is infected or damaged. The dentist cleans the inside of the tooth
and seals it. It is done under local anaesthetic, takes one or two visits, and starts from 280 pounds.
A crown is often fitted afterwards to protect the tooth.
People also ask: what is a root canal, root canal cost, how long is a root canal, does a root canal hurt.

## Teeth whitening
Teeth whitening makes your teeth several shades lighter. The dentist checks your teeth and gums first, and whitening is
not suitable for everyone. The treatment costs 250 pounds and takes about an hour in the chair.
People also ask: whitening, bleaching, whiter teeth, how much is whitening.

## X-rays
Dental X-rays let the dentist see between your teeth and under the gums, where problems cannot be seen by eye.
A single X-ray costs 25 pounds. They are included in the price of a new patient examination.
People also ask: x-ray, dental scan, are x-rays included.

## Insurance and payment
We accept most major dental insurance plans. Please bring your policy details to your appointment.
We take card payments, contactless, and cash. Payment is due on the day of treatment.
We offer a monthly payment plan for treatments over 500 pounds.

## Cancellations
Please give at least 24 hours notice if you need to cancel or move an appointment.
You can cancel by calling the clinic or replying to your reminder text message.
Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours notice, or missed, may be charged a fee of 25 pounds.

## Location and parking
Where to find us: the clinic is at 24 Harbour Road, Brightwell, BW1 2AB, next to the post office.
There is free parking for patients behind the building, and a bus stop directly outside.
The clinic has step-free access and a ground-floor treatment room.

## Children
We welcome children of all ages. Check-ups for children under 18 are free.
We recommend a child's first visit by their first birthday. A parent or guardian must attend with children under 16.

## Emergency appointments
We keep emergency appointment slots free every weekday morning for patients in pain.
To get an emergency slot, call the clinic at 8am. Our urgent dental line is 01632 960 111.
When the clinic is closed, please call NHS 111 for urgent dental help.

## New patients
We are accepting new patients. To register, book a new patient examination by phone or at reception.
Please bring a list of any medicines you take and your previous dentist's details if you have them.
